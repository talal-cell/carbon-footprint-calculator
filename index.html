Climate change is real!!

Our daily activities, such as travelling, using electricity, eating and shopping, can produce carbon emissions. These emissions contribute to climate change and affect the environment.

A carbon footprint is the amount of greenhouse gases produced by our activities. Knowing your carbon footprint can help you make better choices to protect our planet.
